# Pitch Deck — Stil Rehberi

## Amaçİİ

Yatirimci sunumu (Series A / Seed / Pre-Seed) için 20 slide standardi. Sovereign fund ve kurumsal yatirimcilara yönelik.

## Ne Zaman Kullanilir

- Yeni venture yatirimci sunumu
- Mevcut venture round güncelleme
- LP / stratejik yatirimci görÜİşmeleri

## Ton ve Dil

- **Dil:** İngilizce (yatirimci materyali)
- **Ton:** Teknik olarak sağlam, iddial ama abart s z, veri destekli
- **Başliklar:** İddia cÜİmleleri (etiket değil)
- **Slide başina:** 1 ana fikir, 3–5 bullet / 1 grafik / 1 tablo

## 20 Slide Yap isi

01. Cover — Venture name, tagline, VLABS34 emblem  
02. Investment at a glance — KPIs, gate, round, ask  
03. What VLABS34 is + portfolio strip  
04. Divider: "01 THE OPPORTUNITY"  
05. Sector state of play (Bain slide)  
06. Why now — triggers, inflection points  
07. Problem — operational pain, cost, risk  
08. Why it stayed unsolved — technical / structural barriers  
09. Divider: "02 THE COMPANY"  
10. Solution — SEE / DECIDE / ACT / PROVE framework  
11. Technology stack — Physical AI (red) / Cognitive AI (navy)  
12. Third-party proof — cited table (partners, pilots, certifications)  
13. Traction — customers, POCs, booked, pipeline  
14. Market size — TAM / SAM / SOM, geo split  
15. Business model — pricing, unit economics, LTV/CAC  
16. Competition — 2×İÜİ matrix  
17. Defensibility — data, workflow, switching cost, IP  
18. GTM & roadmap — 12–24 month milestones  
19. Team — founders, key hires, advisors  
20. The Ask — round size, use of funds, contact
